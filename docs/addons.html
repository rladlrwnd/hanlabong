Hanlabong Developing

사진 현상, 휠에서 바로.Photo developing, right from the wheel.

현상

Develop

  • 기본 노출·대비·하이라이트·섀도·화이트·블랙, 화이트밸런스.
  • 톤 커브 점을 찍어 밝기를 구간별로.
  • 색조 편집 8색마다 색·채도·밝기.
  • 효과 흑백·비네트·그레인.
  • Basics Exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, white balance.
  • Tone curve Shape brightness point by point.
  • Color mixer Hue, saturation and luminance for 8 colors.
  • Effects B&W, vignette, grain.

휠에서 바로

From the wheel

  • 프리셋 세 개 휠의 "현상" 칸에 놓기만 하면 끝.
  • … 칸 놓으면 슬라이더 창이 열려요. 더블클릭하면 원래 값으로.
  • 내보내기 크기·용량 한도·ppi·출력 선명하게·워터마크·메타데이터.
  • 원본은 그대로 결과는 원본 옆에.
  • Three presets Just drop on the Develop slot.
  • … slot Opens the sliders. Double-click to reset.
  • Export Size, file-size cap, ppi, output sharpening, watermark, metadata.
  • Originals kept Results land beside the original.

Hanlabong (formerly DragEdit) · MIT License. Named after Jeju's hallabong citrus, a nod to Tangerine. The file wheel idea was inspired by DragShift (MIT). Border beam and thinking orbs from libraries.dev (MIT). The wheel on this page is a simulation.
